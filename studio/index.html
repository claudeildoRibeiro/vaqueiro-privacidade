Review, schedule and publish short videos to TikTok.

Vaqueiro Studio is the content studio behind the creator Página di Vaqueiro, a series about everyday life in the Brazilian sertão, set to the creator's own music.

How it works

1

Create

The creator assembles a short vertical video (15–25 s) with their own music and on-screen text.

2

Review

Every video waits in the studio queue. Nothing is posted until the creator previews and approves it.

3

Choose settings

Caption, who can view, comments, Duet and Stitch, and the "AI-generated content" label are chosen for each post.

4

Publish

The approved video is sent to TikTok with Login Kit and the Content Posting API, either posted directly or saved to the TikTok inbox as a draft.

Features

Approval queue

One place to see what is ready, pending or already posted, with the scheduled time of each video.

AI content label

Realistic AI-generated scenes are always published with TikTok's AI-generated content disclosure.

Own music

Videos use only tracks written and released by the creator.

Secure connection

The TikTok account is connected with the official login. Access can be removed at any time in TikTok's settings.

About

Vaqueiro Studio is made by the team behind @paginadivaqueiro on TikTok and Página di Vaqueiro on Facebook. It only handles the creator's own content and account; it does not collect data from TikTok viewers.

Questions or data requests: send a message to Página di Vaqueiro on Facebook.
